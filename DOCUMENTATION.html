Technical Specification & Product Manual

🌐 MeshLink — Off-Grid Peer-to-Peer Messaging

100% Serverless, Decentralized Mesh Messaging & High-Speed Media Sharing for Android without Internet, Cellular Data, or Central Servers.

📌 1. What is MeshLink?

MeshLink is an open-source decentralized messaging app built with Flutter and Native Android Platform Channels. It enables nearby smartphones to communicate directly using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) for background discovery and Wi-Fi Direct (P2P Sockets) for high-speed chat, voice notes, photos, videos, and documents.

🎯 2. Real-World Use Cases

Scenario How MeshLink Solves It
🌪️ Disasters & Emergencies When power grids and cellular towers collapse, rescue teams and families stay connected off-grid.
🏔️ Hiking & Backcountry Zero cellular coverage in remote trails. MeshLink enables group coordination up to 100+ meters.
🏟️ Crowded Stadiums Bypasses congested 4G/5G mobile towers via direct device-to-device wireless links.
✈️ Flights & Underground Metros Allows seamless offline messaging and file transfers in Airplane Mode.
🔒 Private Communications Zero server logs, zero phone numbers, and zero cloud intermediaries.

📱 4. Core Features

  • 💬 WhatsApp-Style Conversations: Clean chat list with media snippets, timestamps, unread indicators, and hold-to-delete message support.
  • 📁 High-Speed Media & File Sharing: In-app video player, universal document viewer (PDF, DOC, ZIP, APK), and Save-to-Downloads.
  • 🛑 In-Flight Cancel Send: Interactive cancel button aborts chunk streaming and completely purges incomplete data from both devices.
  • 🎨 Warm Cream / Deep Dark Themes: Warm Cream/Beige (#F6F3EC) and Sleek Dark (#0B0F14) theme palettes.
  • 📷 Pure QR Pairing: Instant camera scanner and QR card pairing.
  • 🗄️ Embedded Isar NoSQL Storage: Safe offline persistence across app restarts.

❓ 6. FAQ

📡 What is the real-world range?
50 to 100+ meters outdoors in direct line-of-sight; 20 to 40 meters indoors across residential walls.
⚡ How fast are file transfers?
Wi-Fi Direct delivers speeds of 50 Mbps to 250+ Mbps directly device-to-device.
🔋 Does it drain battery quickly?
No. Idle scanning runs on Bluetooth Low Energy consuming less than 15 mW. Wi-Fi Direct only activates during active messaging sessions.
